Method and sources

  • Financial data comes from the SEC's XBRL company facts API (data.sec.gov). Income figures are trailing twelve months: the last full fiscal year plus this year to date, less last year to date.
  • Return on equity and return on tangible equity use average common equity over the last year. Common equity excludes preferred stock. Tangible equity also excludes goodwill and intangible assets.
  • Net revenue is revenue net of interest expense where the company reports it. P/E uses reported GAAP diluted EPS, so it can differ from multiples based on adjusted earnings.
  • Share prices come from Yahoo Finance. Peer median excludes Raymond James.
